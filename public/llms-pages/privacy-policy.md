# Privacy Policy

> Learn how Kitebaze collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal information.

Canonical webpage: [https://kitebaze.com/privacy-policy](https://kitebaze.com/privacy-policy)

Last updated: 2026-09-24

## Page summary

The Privacy Policy explains how Kitebaze handles personal information across its website, client relationships, and configured workflows or services.

## Full public text

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

Kitebaze (“Kitebaze,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) helps businesses improve how work gets done by redesigning workflows, connecting existing tools, automating repetitive tasks, and supporting the systems we build.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when you:

* visit **kitebaze.com**;
* contact us or book a consultation;
* become a Kitebaze client;
* communicate with us;
* use a workflow, automation, form, assistant, integration, or other solution we configure or operate; or
* otherwise interact with our services.

Because Kitebaze often works with systems and information belonging to our clients, our role can vary.

For information we collect directly for our own business purposes, such as website inquiries, client contacts, and billing information, Kitebaze is responsible for that information.

When we process information through a system or workflow on behalf of a client, the client generally determines why that information is being used and Kitebaze processes it to provide the agreed service.

### 1. Information We Collect

#### Information you provide directly

We may collect information you choose to give us, including:

* your name;
* email address;
* telephone number;
* company name;
* job title or role;
* information submitted through forms;
* consultation or project details;
* support requests;
* billing and transaction information; and
* information contained in emails, messages, documents, meetings, or other communications with us.

We only ask for information that is reasonably necessary for the interaction or service.

#### Website and technical information

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

* IP address;
* browser and device information;
* operating system;
* pages visited;
* referring pages or websites;
* approximate location derived from technical information;
* dates and times of visits; and
* interactions with our website.

We may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand how our website is being used and to improve it.

### 2. Information We Process While Working With Clients

Kitebaze builds around the systems and processes a business already uses.

Depending on the engagement, this can mean working with information contained in:

* email and inboxes;
* spreadsheets;
* CRM systems;
* accounting or invoicing software;
* calendars;
* forms;
* customer-support systems;
* messaging platforms;
* project-management systems;
* payment or billing systems;
* documents and file storage;
* scheduling tools; and
* other business systems selected by the client.

As a result, a Kitebaze workflow may process personal information relating to a client's customers, prospects, employees, contractors, suppliers, or other business contacts.

Examples may include names, email addresses, telephone numbers, appointment details, correspondence, invoices, customer records, service information, documents, and other information required for the workflow.

The exact information processed depends on the workflow being built and the systems involved.

We aim to process only the information reasonably required to make that workflow work.

### 3. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

* respond to inquiries;
* provide consultations and workflow reviews;
* understand a business process or bottleneck;
* design and implement workflows;
* connect systems and applications;
* operate automations;
* configure forms, notifications, reminders, and communications;
* update records across connected systems;
* provide support and maintenance;
* troubleshoot problems;
* manage our relationship with clients;
* process payments and administer contracts;
* improve the reliability and quality of our services;
* maintain the security of our systems;
* prevent misuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity;
* meet legal, regulatory, tax, and accounting requirements; and
* communicate with you about Kitebaze where permitted by law.

We will not intentionally use personal information for purposes that are materially different from those for which it was collected without an appropriate legal basis or, where required, additional consent.

### 4. Connected Tools and Third-Party Services

Kitebaze does not require every client to replace the software they already use.

Our work frequently involves connecting or configuring third-party products selected by us or by the client.

These may include hosting providers, cloud platforms, email systems, automation tools, CRM platforms, accounting software, communications services, analytics providers, form tools, databases, and other business applications.

When a workflow requires information to move between those services, information may be transmitted to or processed by the relevant providers.

Each third-party provider may also process information according to its own terms, privacy policy, and contractual relationship with Kitebaze or the client.

We evaluate the systems involved in each engagement and aim to limit access and data movement to what is reasonably required for the workflow.

### 5. AI and Automated Processing

Some Kitebaze workflows may use artificial intelligence or machine-learning services when those tools are appropriate for the work being performed.

For example, this may include helping a workflow interpret documents, categorize information, extract information from text, prepare draft content, route requests, or process unstructured business information.

Where an external AI service is used, information needed to perform that task may be transmitted to the relevant provider.

The use of AI depends on the particular workflow. Kitebaze does not assume that every process requires AI and may instead use conventional automation or integrations where they are more appropriate.

We design workflows so that decisions, exceptions, approvals, or judgment that should remain with a person can remain under human control.

### 6. Client Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate authority, consent, or other lawful basis to provide Kitebaze with access to personal information used in their workflows.

Clients are also responsible for determining which employees, contractors, systems, and information Kitebaze should be permitted to access.

Where Kitebaze processes personal information on behalf of a client, we use that information to provide the agreed services and according to the applicable agreement with that client.

### 7. How We Share Information

We may disclose personal information to service providers where reasonably necessary to operate Kitebaze or provide our services.

These may include providers that assist with:

* cloud hosting;
* databases and storage;
* automation infrastructure;
* communications;
* analytics;
* payment processing;
* accounting;
* customer support;
* security;
* artificial intelligence services; and
* other technology required for a client's workflow.

We may also disclose information:

* where you or the relevant client directs us to;
* where necessary to complete a requested service;
* to professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants, or insurers;
* where required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or other lawful process;
* where reasonably necessary to protect Kitebaze, our clients, users, or others from fraud, misuse, security threats, or harm; or
* in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale, or transfer of all or part of our business.

Kitebaze does not sell personal information as part of our business model.

### 8. International Processing and Data Location

Kitebaze is based in Canada, but some of the technology providers used by Kitebaze or our clients may operate infrastructure in other countries.

As a result, personal information may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in jurisdictions outside your province or outside Canada.

Information processed in another jurisdiction may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

Where appropriate, we consider data location, security, contractual protections, and the nature of the information when selecting or configuring providers.

### 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information we handle.

These may include measures such as:

* access controls;
* authentication;
* limiting access to people who require it;
* encrypted connections;
* secure cloud infrastructure;
* system permissions;
* credential management;
* monitoring and logging;
* backups where appropriate; and
* limiting the information collected or retained.

No internet-based system or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Clients should not provide Kitebaze with access to information that is unnecessary for the agreed workflow.

### 10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to provide our services, to meet contractual obligations, or to satisfy legal, accounting, security, or regulatory requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on:

* the type of information;
* the client engagement;
* the system in which the information is stored;
* contractual requirements;
* legal requirements; and
* whether the information is required for security, dispute resolution, or recordkeeping.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it.

Information contained in a client's own systems may remain subject to that client's retention policies even after Kitebaze's engagement ends.

### 11. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate properly, remember preferences, measure website performance, and understand how visitors use the site.

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser.

Disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of the website function.

Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

### 12. Marketing Communications

If you request information from us, become a client, or otherwise provide appropriate consent, we may send you communications about Kitebaze services, updates, or related information.

We comply with applicable requirements governing commercial electronic communications.

You can unsubscribe from promotional emails using the unsubscribe option included in the message or by contacting us.

We may still send communications that are necessary to provide services, administer an account, respond to a request, or manage an existing client relationship.

### 13. Access, Correction and Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights relating to personal information about you, including the ability to:

* request access to your personal information;
* ask us to correct inaccurate information;
* withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
* ask questions about how your information is used or disclosed; or
* request deletion of certain information where applicable.

Some requests may be limited where we are legally or contractually required to retain information.

If the information is being processed by Kitebaze on behalf of one of our clients, we may direct your request to that client because they control the underlying business relationship and information.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a privacy request.

### 14. Children's Privacy

Kitebaze's website and services are designed for businesses and are not directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our website for the purpose of offering Kitebaze services to them.

If a client's workflow involves information relating to minors, the client is responsible for ensuring that the information can lawfully be provided and processed.

### 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Kitebaze, our services, our technology, or applicable requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

If a change materially affects how we handle personal information, we may provide additional notice where appropriate.

Questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Kitebaze's handling of personal information can be directed to:
