The Insights deal progress report allows you to view the movement of your deals through stages within a specified time frame, showing how well your deals are progressing through your sales cycle.
Creating your progress report
To create a deal progress report, go to Insights , then click “+ Create” > Report > Deal > Progress.
Note: With the introduction of the option to archive deals and leads, both archived and non-archived data are included by default in the reports. If you’d like to exclude either type, use the available filtering options to customize your view.
Viewing your progress report
Note: Progress reports use the most recent updates to deal stages, meaning if a deal is moved from Stage 2 back to Stage 1, the deal is represented in Stage 1.
The progress report by default shows the following filters:
Date of entering stage > is > this year
Pipeline > is > [your default pipeline]
You can always adjust or add filters according to the data you want to see, but the d ate of entering stage filter is locked in since it‘s needed to assess deal progress.
How is deal progress measured?
Note: Winning a deal doesn’t affect deal progress results in the stages that were skipped.
If you measure by the number of deals , you’ll see the number of deals that have entered each stage in the timeframe selected.
Hovering over each column will show the breakdown of the field you have chosen to segment by.
Note: The entered stage deal count also includes deals that have skipped that stage while moving on to a future stage. For example, if a deal has moved from stage two directly to stage five in the time frame selected, it will show as having entered stages three, four and five.
