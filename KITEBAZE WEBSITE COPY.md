### Lead → Job

#### Stop letting new work sit in an inbox.

A customer submits a form, sends an email or makes an inquiry.

Kitebaze can capture the information, organize the request, identify what's missing, create the customer or job record and trigger the next step automatically.

**Less chasing. Faster response. Cleaner intake.**

---

### Field → Office

#### Get information out of the field without the paperwork.

A technician finishes a job and sends photos, notes or a voice message.

Kitebaze can turn that messy field information into structured job notes, customer updates, internal tasks or documentation.

**Your field team does the job. The system handles the admin around it.**

---

### Job → Cash

#### Finished work shouldn't create another pile of work.

When a job is completed, Kitebaze can trigger the next operational steps — documentation, invoice preparation, customer communication, payment reminders and internal updates.

**Get from completed job to completed paperwork faster.**

---

### Quote → Follow-up

#### Stop losing work because nobody followed up.

Quotes can trigger scheduled follow-ups, customer reminders and internal alerts based on what happens next.

Your team knows who needs attention without searching through yesterday's messages.

### Documents → Data

#### Stop typing information that's already sitting in a document.

Invoices, PDFs, forms, receipts and emails can be read, organized and routed into the systems or workflows that need them.

# FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Kitebaze another software platform?

No.

Kitebaze builds automation around your operation. Whenever possible, we connect and extend the tools you already use rather than asking you to replace everything.

---

### Do we have to replace our current software?

Usually not.

If a tool works well for part of your business, we keep it there. The opportunity is often in connecting it to everything happening before and after it.

If something truly is the bottleneck, we'll tell you.

---

### What kinds of work can you automate?

Common opportunities include customer intake, lead routing, quote follow-up, scheduling, reminders, job updates, document processing, data entry, internal notifications, invoicing workflows and connecting information between systems.

But we start by studying your workflow rather than selling you a predefined automation.

---

### Is everything powered by AI?

No — deliberately.

AI is useful when work involves messy information, documents, conversations or judgment.

Straightforward repetitive tasks are often better handled by normal automation.

We use the simplest reliable solution for the job.

---

### What happens first?

We start with a Workflow Review.

You show us how a process works today, including the software, spreadsheets, emails, manual steps and workarounds involved.

We identify the bottlenecks and determine where automation would create the most value.

---

### Can we start with one small problem?

Absolutely.

In fact, that can be the best way to start.

We can automate one repeated bottleneck, put it into real use and expand from there when it makes sense.

---

### Do we need a technical team?

No.

Our job is to deal with the integrations, automation and technical implementation.

Your job is to show us how the business actually works.

---

### What happens when something changes?

Business processes change and software changes.

Kitebaze systems are built to be maintained and adjusted rather than handed over as a fragile one-off automation.

We can also provide ongoing support for workflows that become important to your operation.

---

### Who is Kitebaze for?

We're built for owner-led and growing businesses where too much work is still being moved manually.

Especially trades, contractors, field-service businesses and other companies whose real workflows don't always fit neatly inside off-the-shelf software.
